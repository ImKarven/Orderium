# Derivative Content
Orderium is derivative of other projects.

## Update
Updates to this disclosure and the derivative contents only happen when Orderium releases a new version.
Such updates are mentioned in the changelog of the new version.
<br>
Such updates are only applied on that new version of Orderium.
<br>
Therefore, the disclosure and derivative contents are the same as the one you viewed at the time of downloading your current Orderium version, even if there is a new one.
<br>
This applies after September 27, 2026, which is when Modrinth starts enforcing their new AI policy and project disclosures system.

## [Paper](https://papermc.io/software/paper/)
This should be pretty obvious. Without Paper, the plugin is basically useless.

## [ConfigurationMaster](https://github.com/Errored-Innovations/ConfigurationMaster)
Orderium uses ConfigurationMaster as a library to manage the configuration files of Orderium.
<br>
All code from ConfigurationMaster is included in Orderium.

## [packetevents](https://packetevents.com/)
Orderium interacts with packetevents to listen to certain incoming packets.
